    OEM & Custom Manufacturing

    OEM Brass & Bronze Components for Firefighting Equipment Manufacturers

    BlazeBastion supplies OEM brass and bronze components to firefighting equipment manufacturers. We work directly from your drawings and specifications — castings, machining, finishing and assembly are custom-built and managed through our vetted network of partner factories, with full material certifications and one point of contact throughout the project.

    Capabilities

    Brass & Bronze Castings

    • Sand casting and gravity die casting of copper alloys
    • Typical parts: valve bodies, adapters, couplings, nozzle bodies, sprinkler bases, hydrant components and fittings
    • Surface finish: natural, polished, machined; plating and coating on request

    CNC Machining to Your Drawings

    • CNC turning and machining centers, produced to your drawing tolerances
    • Drawings accepted in DWG / DXF / STEP / IGES / PDF
    • Threads to your specification — NPT, BSP, BSPT, NST, Storz and other fire-service standards
    • First Article Inspection (FAI) before volume production

    Materials & Certifications

    GradeTypeTypical Applications
    C36000Free-cutting brass (bar & rod)Machined adapters, couplings, inserts, threaded fittings
    C83600Cast red brass (85-5-5-5)Valve bodies, low-pressure fittings, cast components
    C95400Aluminum bronzeHigh-strength components, seawater and marine service
    LG2 (BS 1400)GunmetalFire service valves, hydrant and riser fittings

    Full material certifications — chemical composition and mechanical test reports — are provided with shipments on request. We are happy to sign an NDA before receiving your drawings or samples.
