    FIRE BLANKET
    Fire Blanket is a type of specially treated fiberglass satin texture.It is a simple fire control
    tool to put out fire. Fire Blanket is widely used in corporation,supermarket, shipping, car
    and house etc，especially used in kitchen,hotel,recrestion ground,gas station etc that
    can easy to be on fire area, react on put out on fire, prevention and cure the fire
    overspread, protection and save people life.
    WELDING BL ANKET
    Welding blankets are surface treated fiberglass fabrics with heat-resistance,high
    thickness and tight weaving, suitable for isolation and prevention in industrial and civil
    welding operation.According to the size of operation area, it can hang the blanket like
    curtain to isolate welding area and spread on the floor to effectively prevent the ifluence
    to people or goods at neighboring area caused by high-temperature welding sparks.
